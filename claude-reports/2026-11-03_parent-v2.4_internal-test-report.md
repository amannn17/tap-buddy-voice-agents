## Summary

31 of the 35 steps that were marked passed. That includes most of the problems found in round 2: the hold bug, quiet replies, interruptions and teach-back scoring. There are three things to fix, and they come down to two causes:

- **A question the bot can't answer ends the call.** This happened with an OTP question and with an English question about messages.
- **Silence doesn't move the call on.** When the parent went quiet, the bot waited.

## What's now working

- **The hold bug is fixed** (call 3). The bot waited for the parent to come to the phone instead of hanging up.
- **Quiet "हाँ" and "जी" were heard** first time (calls 1 and 6). A tester on a clear line is easier to hear than a real parent, so this still needs checking in the pilot.
- **Interruptions:** the bot stopped, answered the question and carried on from where it was (call 3).
- **Teach-back:** the correction came once, with no re-test, and the first answer was the one scored (calls 6 and 7).
- **The three-unknowns cap:** after the third UNKNOWN, the call went straight to the close (call 5).
- **Reassurance:** the "credit card" suspicion and "is this a machine?" were handled (calls 2 and 7).
- **English:** the bot switched to English when asked and stayed in it (call 4).

### Two hang-ups, one cause

In calls 2 and 4, a question the bot couldn't answer ended the call. A parent would hear that as being hung up on. The fix is a rule listing the only reasons the bot may end a call:

- a wrong number;
- an opt-out;
- a safety concern;
- a callback request;
- silence after the re-prompt;
- the normal close.

For any other question, the bot answers it or uses UNKNOWN, then carries on.

### Silence may explain the round 2 Step 1 drops

If the bot waits when a parent goes quiet, the call stalls until the parent hangs up. That would explain the long Step 1 calls in round 2 that ended in a hang-up (median 37 s) and were summarised as "no response recorded". Check this on the "no answer to the identity question" calls in the parent listening list.

## Proposed changes (`parent-v2.5`, to agree before building)

1. **When the call may end.** The bot ends the call only in the cases listed above. Any other question gets an answer or the UNKNOWN line, then the script carries on.
2. **Fraud or OTP line.** No OTP, bank details or payment is ever asked for, and the parent can check with the school. Then carry on. The teacher prompt already has a matching line to align with.
3. **English UNKNOWN line.** Say the same meaning as the Hindi UNKNOWN line when the call has switched to English.
4. **Busy or exams at the commitment:** "बिल्कुल, exams ज़्यादा ज़रूरी हैं। {{student_name}} को best of luck! जब समय मिले, तब याद दिला दीजिएगा। धन्यवाद।" Log the commitment as unclear.

**Configuration (VocalLabs, not the prompt):** turn on the no-response or idle timeout, about 3 to 5 s, and confirm that it gives the bot a turn so the silence rules can run.
